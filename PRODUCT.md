# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sploot is built first for one person whose memes are scattered across camera
rolls, screenshots, downloads, bookmarks, and group chats. They are usually
trying to save something quickly, retrieve it from a fuzzy memory, or send it
back to a conversation without maintaining folders or tags.

## Product Purpose

Sploot is a private multimedia meme library that makes capture, semantic
retrieval, shuffle, related-media discovery, and sharing feel immediate. The
product succeeds when the user's own media is easy to see, easy to recover, and
never subordinated to decorative interface chrome.

## Brand Personality

Goofy, blunt, and personal. Sploot should feel like a capable image workbench
owned by someone terminally online: deadpan rather than corporate, lively
without becoming a meme casino, and private by default.

## Anti-references

- Generic centered SaaS and AI-search landing pages.
- Social feeds, creator dashboards, and engagement-maximizing meme products.
- Neo-brutalism applied indiscriminately to every icon and compact control.
- Decorative fake windows, hand-drawn placeholder figures, and UI chrome that
  covers or crops the media it is meant to serve.
- Motion that shifts navigation geometry or makes routine controls theatrical.

## Design Principles

1. The meme is the product. Preserve the complete media and keep metadata and
   actions out of its way.
2. Quiet chrome, feral contents. Structural borders and color carry hierarchy;
   compact controls stay restrained and familiar.
3. Show shipped truth. Labs, demos, copy, and screenshots must faithfully
   represent the real product and use credible media fixtures.
4. Keep repeated work fast. Search, upload, shuffle, filtering, and sharing
   should remain stable, dense, and immediately legible.
5. Spend personality deliberately. One memorable beat beats decoration on
   every surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast in light and dark themes. Every action must be keyboard
reachable with a visible focus state; touch targets remain at least 44px on
mobile. Reduced-motion preferences are respected, state is never communicated
by color alone, and media is shown without destructive cropping.
