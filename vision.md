# Vision

## One-Liner
Personal meme library with semantic search and shuffle - find your memes with words, not folders.

## North Star
The meme app that knows your taste. Search naturally, shuffle endlessly, and eventually generate new memes tailored to what you find funny.

## Key Differentiators
- **Semantic search**: Find memes with natural language, not tags or filenames
- **Shuffle button**: Surprisingly rare - just show me random memes from my collection
- **Bangers ranking**: Surface your best memes, not just recent ones
- **Related memes**: "More like this" within your own library
- **Taste-based generation**: Future - AI creates memes matching your humor profile

## Target User
People with meme collections scattered across Twitter bookmarks, Google Photos, camera rolls. Not creators or marketers - just people who save too many memes and want to actually find them again.

## Current Focus
Core experience: save, search, shuffle. Make the basics delightful before adding generation.

## Future Ideas
- **AI meme generation**: Analyze user's bangers → synthesize commonalities → generate new memes matching their taste
- **Multimedia support**: Short videos, GIFs - memes aren't just images anymore
- **Share optimization**: Easy export/share to messaging apps

## Vibe
Goofy. Fun. Personal. Not taking itself too seriously.
