lab-053 — stranger-to-aha mechanism

card sploot-053 · goal: a stranger goes landing → working plain-words search in < 5 minutes · 7 structurally distinct mechanisms · delegated-mode decision recorded below

Constraint set: the aha ("type words → get the picture") requires a populated, embedded library. A fresh account has neither. The landing hero already fakes it honestly (token-overlap sim over doodles, labeled). What's missing is the first real semantic search, on real product machinery, inside the first five minutes — including for a stranger who has zero memes on hand.

One button in the first-run capture rig (lane A, next to the demo pile). It ingests 8 bundled, license-safe, original meme images through the real upload pipeline (blob, dedupe, tags), writes their precomputed real CLIP vectors (committed manifest, built once against the production model), warms the text-embedding cache for the suggested first queries, then routes straight into a live search.

Why it wins: real product machinery end-to-end (pgvector cosine search, match/near states) with zero Replicate calls on the seed path — no rate limit, no cron, no 5-minute wait, deterministic under 10 seconds. Works for a stranger with no memes on hand. Opt-in respects the private-library premise; the starter-pile tag makes the whole pile one sweep to delete. Smallest new surface: one route + one empty-state lane.

A wizard that walks the new user through uploading 2–3 of their own images with sync_embeddings=true (the upload API already supports it), then prompts a first search over them.

Why it loses as the primary: it presumes the stranger has memes on hand at the desk they signed up from — often false, and the demo dies there. It also puts live Replicate latency + rate-limit/budget gates (throttled uploads silently fall back to the 5-min cron sweep) inside the 5-minute promise. Right as a follow-up for the user's own first uploads; wrong as the guaranteed path.
